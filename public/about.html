Where Curiosity Sparks Creativity

Personalized enrichment for grades 5-12 — live online and in-person.

Excelsior Enrichment Program (EEP) helps students in grades 5 through 12 grow beyond the standard classroom — through small, personalized classes in math, science, coding, English, debate, and public speaking, plus focused test prep. We built EEP around one idea: kids learn best when they feel seen, challenged at the right level, and genuinely curious about what they're studying.

Every class is taught by a real instructor, not a pre-recorded video or a script. Whether your child joins us live online or in person, they get an instructor who adapts to how they learn, tracks their progress, and keeps sessions small enough that no one gets lost in the crowd.

We offer flexible scheduling because families are busy — live sessions with options that fit around school, sports, and everything else on your child's plate. Our goal in every class is simple: build real confidence and real skill, not just a checked box.

What we teach

  • Math and science enrichment for grades 5-12
  • Coding classes, from beginner to building real projects
  • English, debate, and public speaking
  • Standardized test prep (SAT/ACT and grade-level exams)

Our approach

Small classes, real instructors, and a schedule that works for your family — whether that's a weekly live-online session or an in-person class near you. Browse our current class catalog to see what's running now, or check our events calendar for upcoming workshops and seasonal programs.

Frequently asked questions

What grades do you serve?

Students in grades 5 through 12.

Do you offer both online and in-person classes?

Yes — each class listing shows whether it's live online or in-person, so you can filter to whichever fits your family.

How do I enroll my child?

Create a free account, browse classes on the homepage, and click "Enroll" on any class with open spots.
