Who we are

Where curiosity sparks creativity

Excelsior Enrichment Program helps students in grades 3 through 12 grow beyond the standard classroom, with small, personalized classes in math, science, coding, English, debate, and public speaking, plus focused test prep.

We built EEP around one idea: kids learn best when they feel seen, are challenged at the right level, and are curious about what they're studying.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation to sign up for anything afterwards.

What happens during the assessment?

Your child does a skills check in the subject you choose, and we talk with you about your child's goals. Afterwards we share a recommendation for next steps.

What grades do you serve?

Students in grades 3 through 12, whether they're catching up, keeping pace, or ready for a challenge.

Do you offer both online and in-person classes?

Yes. Each class is either live online or in person, so you can choose whichever fits your family.

Who teaches the classes?

Every class is taught live by a real instructor, not a pre-recorded video. Instructors adapt to how your child learns and track their progress.

How do I enroll after the assessment?

We'll recommend classes that fit. You can enroll by creating a free account on our website, browsing classes, and clicking "Enroll" on any class with open spots. We're also happy to help over the phone.

When are you open?

9:00 AM – 8:00 PM, every day. We'll find an assessment time that fits your schedule.
